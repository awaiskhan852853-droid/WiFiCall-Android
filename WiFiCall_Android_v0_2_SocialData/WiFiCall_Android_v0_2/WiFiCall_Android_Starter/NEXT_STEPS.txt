WiFi Call - Development Roadmap

MVP-1 (current):
Android UI prototype. No real calls.

MVP-2:
- Authentication and unique user IDs
- WebRTC audio calling
- Signaling backend
- STUN/TURN servers
- Incoming call handling

MVP-3:
- Admin dashboard
- Abuse/spam reporting and blocking
- Privacy policy and terms
- Secure account recovery

Business release:
- Telecom/SIP provider integration for phone-number termination
- Per-country pricing and cost controls
- Wallet ledger and payment provider
- Identity, fraud, regulatory and legal review
- Monitoring, support and service-level planning

Do not sell or advertise phone-number calling until the telecom provider,
legal permissions, pricing, emergency-call limitations and compliance are verified.
